Description
Cycle-aware strength training for women who lift. Sundee Fundee helps you plan workouts around your menstrual cycle, recovery, pain, and energy so training feels clear and consistent.

BUILD A COACH PLAN
Choose your time, focus, energy, and equipment. Sundee Fundee builds a Coach Plan with exercises, sets, reps, rest, and suggested substitutions, whether you train in a full gym, with dumbbells, with kettlebells, or at home.

CYCLE AND PERIOD CONTEXT
Track periods, view cycle phase estimates, and get workout guidance that may adjust volume, intensity, and exercise selection based on your logged menstrual cycle and available Health data. HealthKit permissions are optional, and the app still works without Apple Health access.

STRUCTURED STRENGTH PROGRAMS
Follow strength programs with clear sessions and built-in progression. Use programs when you want structure, or build a one-off workout when you need flexibility.

RECOVERY AND PAIN TOOLS
Log pain, track recovery context, and use pain-aware exercise swaps to keep training practical. Sundee Fundee is a fitness tool, not a medical device, and it does not diagnose or treat injuries.

PROGRESS TRACKING
Track one-rep maxes, barbell lifts, benchmarks, PRs, weekly workouts, training volume, and challenge progress. See what is improving without digging through spreadsheets or paper notes.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Continue as a guest for local storage, or sign in with Apple to sync your workouts, maxes, cycle logs, and progress with iCloud. There are no ads and all features are free.

Notes
Sundee Fundee is a free health & fitness app for cycle-aware strength training.

SIGNING IN:
- Tap "Continue as Guest" on the sign-in screen to use the app without an Apple ID.
- Alternatively, "Sign in with Apple" enables iCloud sync across devices.
- Guest mode uses local storage; all features work without sign-in.

KEY FEATURES TO TEST:
- Today tab: View stats, Coach Plan suggestions, coaching insights, and shortcuts.
- Workouts tab: Browse workouts and tap "Build Coach Plan" to create a personalized session.
- Programs tab: View structured training programs.
- Cycle tab: Log periods, view cycle phase, and access pain and recovery tools.
- Progress tab: Open maxes, benchmarks, analytics, challenges, and export.
- Settings: Configure cycle tracking, weight units, experience level, and goals.

HEALTHKIT:
The app requests HealthKit access for workout data and menstrual cycle tracking to adapt training recommendations. Granting permissions is optional; the app works without HealthKit.

DATA & PRIVACY:
- Export: Progress -> Export (exports all user data as JSON).
- Privacy Policy: Settings -> Privacy Policy link.
- Account Deletion: Settings -> Delete All Data & Account.

All features are free. There are no in-app purchases.
