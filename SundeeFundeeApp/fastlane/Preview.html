Description
Train smarter, not just harder. Sundee Fundee is the strength training app that adapts to your body's hormonal cycle, so every workout hits at the right intensity.

CYCLE-AWARE TRAINING
Sync with Apple Health to automatically adjust your training recommendations based on your menstrual cycle phase. Whether you're in your follicular peak or luteal phase, Sundee Fundee tailors volume and intensity so you train with your body, not against it.

STRUCTURED PROGRAMS
Follow proven strength programs with built-in progression. Programs are delivered fresh and updated regularly, so you always have a structured path forward.

AI WORKOUT BUILDER
Generate personalized workouts powered by on-device AI. Tell it your goals, available equipment, and time — get a complete session designed for you.

SMART INJURY MANAGEMENT
Log injuries and let the app automatically modify exercises. Track pain trends over time and get phase-appropriate session recommendations to keep you moving safely.

ONE-REP MAX TRACKING
Log and track your maxes across all major lifts. See your progress over time with clear, visual history.

BENCHMARK TESTING
Test yourself against standardized benchmarks — from timed workouts to max-rep challenges. Track your scores and watch yourself improve.

SYNC EVERYWHERE
Sign in with Apple and your data syncs seamlessly across all your devices via iCloud. Your workouts, maxes, and progress are always with you.

PRIVACY FIRST
Your health data stays on your device and in your private iCloud account. We never sell your data or use advertising trackers. All AI processing happens on-device.

Notes
Sundee Fundee is a free health & fitness app for cycle-aware strength training.

SIGNING IN:
- Tap "Continue as Guest" on the sign-in screen to use the app without an Apple ID.
- Alternatively, "Sign in with Apple" enables iCloud sync across devices.
- Guest mode uses local storage — all features work without sign-in.

KEY FEATURES TO TEST:
- Dashboard: View stats, suggested AI workouts, coaching insights, and shortcuts.
- Workouts tab: Browse workouts and tap "Generate AI Workout" to create a personalized session.
- Programs tab: View structured training programs.
- Maxes tab: Log and track one-rep max lifts.
- Analytics tab: View training charts and progress.
- Benchmarks tab: Test against standardized fitness benchmarks.
- Settings: Configure cycle tracking, weight units, experience level, and goals.

HEALTHKIT:
The app requests HealthKit access for workout data and menstrual cycle tracking to adapt training recommendations. Granting permissions is optional — the app works without HealthKit.

DATA & PRIVACY:
- Export: Settings → Export My Data (exports all user data as JSON).
- Privacy Policy: Settings → Privacy Policy link.
- Account Deletion: Settings → Delete All Data & Account.

All features are free. No in-app purchases or subscriptions.
