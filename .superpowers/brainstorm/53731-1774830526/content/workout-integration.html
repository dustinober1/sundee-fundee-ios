Why the adjustment?
During the luteal phase, progesterone rises which may affect recovery and thermoregulation. We've reduced load by 3% and volume by 8% to match your body's needs. Toggle off if you're feeling strong today.

Integration Points

  1. Phase banner — appears at top of program detail + AI workout pages when cycle tracking is active
  2. Adjusted values — shows modified sets/reps/% with small "was X" annotation
  3. Toggle override — on AI workout page, lets user disable adjustments if feeling strong
  4. Educational callout — explains why adjustments are made, specific to current phase
  5. Phase icons — ⚡ ovulation, 🌱 follicular, 🩸 menstrual, 🌙 luteal
