Cycle Phase Banner — Layout Options

Where should the cycle phase info appear on the dashboard?

A

Below Welcome Header

Full-width banner right after "Hey, [name]". Most prominent placement — cycle awareness is front and center.

B

Above Stat Cards

Between the welcome and the 3 stat cards. Balanced visibility without being the very first thing.

C

As a Card (same level as Quick Actions)

Styled like the Quick Actions card. Less prominent but consistent with the card-based layout.

What info should it show?

1

Phase Name

e.g., "Follicular Phase" or "Ovulation Phase"

2

Training Recommendation

e.g., "Peak performance — go for PRs!"

3

Cycle Day

e.g., "Day 14 of 28"

4

Days Until Next Phase

e.g., "3 days until Luteal"
