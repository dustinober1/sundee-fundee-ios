2.0 Delivery Roadmap

A 14-week platform release, built as independently verifiable vertical slices.

Weeks
1–2

Foundation and shadow assessment

Daily context, personal-baseline contracts, readiness assessment, confidence, reason codes, records, cache, and scoring fixtures. Run against test data before it changes workouts.

GateInvariants and scenario suite pass; no user-visible adaptation yet.
Weeks
3–5

Daily readiness experience

Today state and score, detailed breakdown, maximum-signal check-in, HealthKit integration, missing-data UX, local cache, history, and refreshed readiness widget.

GateGuest, denied, sparse, offline, and high-confidence flows verified.
Weeks
6–9

Bounded adaptive training

Decision packets, program-aware session changes, deload and active-recovery policies, adaptation preview, explanations, original-session restore, and completion feedback.

GateEvery adaptation stays in bounds, has reasons, and is reversible.
Weeks
10–12

Program, progress, and social intelligence

Missed-session reshuffling, planned vs reactive deloads, readiness-to-performance insights, monthly review upgrades, milestones, and privacy-safe sharing.

GateLong-term program intent remains intact across daily changes.
Weeks
13–14

Reliability and release hardening

Focused SyncQueue activation, CloudKit deployment, accessibility, performance, model-version migration, privacy audit, simulator journeys, release notes, and rollback plan.

GateFull release gate passes; upload or submission still requires explicit approval.
Success measures
Better decisions

At least 75% of completed adapted sessions are rated “right for today,” with zero known out-of-bounds adaptations.

Better retention

Target a 10% relative lift in second-week workout completion and four-week returning trainers versus the 1.7 baseline.

Stronger value

At least 70% positive usefulness feedback for readiness explanations and Coach Plans, while maintaining or improving store sentiment.
