## Cross-cutting observations

1. **CloudKit call reduction is a recurring theme** — P-M1 (Dashboard), P-M2 (Maxes), and the R-M4 decode-failure count all suggest the app could benefit from a thin caching layer or a shared `UserSettings`/`WorkoutsCache` `@MainActor` class held at the app level.
2. **Accessibility + Art Deco consistency overlap** — U-M10, U-M12, and the `AppTheme.Typography.*` patterns that already exist suggest one sweep across icon sizes + color tokens could close many UX findings in one pass.
3. **Haptics are near-absent** — only 1 haptic call exists today. Adding 5 well-placed haptics is a high-leverage, low-risk change.
4. **Reliability findings are all about visibility, not data loss** — SyncQueue, decode failures, revoked credentials all have correct underlying behavior but fail to surface signal to the user. Settings diagnostics row would solve ~3 of these at once.
5. **Guest migration (R-M3) is the highest user-impact reliability gap** — real user data loss risk if a guest accumulates history then signs in. Strongly favor must-fix.
