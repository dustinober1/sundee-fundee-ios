## Description (4000 char max)

Train smarter, not just harder. Sundee Fundee is the strength training app that adapts to your body's hormonal cycle, so every workout hits at the right intensity.

CYCLE-AWARE TRAINING
Sync with Apple Health to automatically adjust your training recommendations based on your menstrual cycle phase. Whether you're in your follicular peak or luteal phase, Sundee Fundee tailors volume and intensity so you train with your body, not against it.

STRUCTURED PROGRAMS
Follow proven strength programs with built-in progression. Programs are delivered fresh and updated regularly, so you always have a structured path forward.

WORKOUT OF THE DAY
Never wonder what to do at the gym. Daily WODs keep your training varied and challenging, with exercises matched to your current cycle phase and injury status.

AI WORKOUT BUILDER
Generate personalized workouts powered by AI. Tell it your goals, available equipment, and time — get a complete session designed for you.

SMART INJURY MANAGEMENT
Log injuries and let the app automatically modify exercises. Track pain trends over time and get phase-appropriate rehab session recommendations to keep you moving safely.

ONE-REP MAX TRACKING
Log and track your maxes across all major lifts. See your progress over time with clear, visual history.

BENCHMARK TESTING
Test yourself against standardized benchmarks — from timed workouts to max-rep challenges. Track your scores and watch yourself improve.

SYNC EVERYWHERE
Sign in with Apple and your data syncs seamlessly across all your devices via iCloud. Your workouts, maxes, and progress are always with you.

BEAUTIFUL DESIGN
An Art Deco-inspired interface that makes tracking your training a pleasure, not a chore.

FREE TO USE
Core features are completely free. Upgrade to Sundee Plus ($4.99/mo) for enhanced training intelligence with pain trends and effort insights, or Sundee Premium ($9.99/mo) for unlimited AI workouts, rehab sessions, and data export.

PRIVACY FIRST
Your health data stays on your device and in your private iCloud account. We never sell your data or use advertising trackers.
