Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sync my data across devices?
Sign in with Apple to enable iCloud sync. Your workouts, maxes, and progress will automatically sync across all your devices.

How does cycle-aware training work?
Grant HealthKit access in Settings, and Sundee Fundee reads your cycle data to recommend training intensity and volume adjustments based on your current phase.

Is the Rucking Club app really free?
Yes. All Rucking Club features are included at no cost — GPS tracking, clubs, leaderboards, badges, events, advanced stats, CSV export, custom app icons, and Apple Watch support. No paywalls, no subscriptions.

How do I report an injury?
In Sundee Fundee (strength training), go to your profile or settings and use the injury management feature. The app will automatically modify exercises to accommodate your injury.

How do I delete my account?
In Sundee Fundee Rucking Club, open the app, go to the More tab, and tap Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, ruck history, leaderboard entries, and club memberships. You can also email us at support@sundeefundee.com for assistance.
