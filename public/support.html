Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sync my data across devices?
Sign in with Apple to enable iCloud sync. Your workouts, maxes, and progress will automatically sync across all your devices.

How does cycle-aware training work?
Grant HealthKit access in Settings, and Sundee Fundee reads your cycle data to recommend training intensity and volume adjustments based on your current phase.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through Apple. Go to Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions on your device to modify or cancel.

Can I use the app without a subscription?
Yes. Core features including workout logging, max tracking, and programs are available for free. Subscriptions unlock premium features like AI workout generation and advanced analytics.

How do I report an injury?
Go to your profile or settings and use the injury management feature. The app will automatically modify exercises to accommodate your injury.

How do I delete my data?
Delete the app to remove local data. For iCloud data, visit Settings > Apple ID > iCloud on your device. You can also email us for assistance.
