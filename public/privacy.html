Privacy Policy

Sundee Fundee

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data Sundee Fundee collects, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

Account Information

When you sign in with Apple, we receive your Apple-provided user identifier. We do not receive or store your email address unless you choose to share it. You may also use the app in guest mode without creating an account.

Health & Fitness Data

With your explicit permission, Sundee Fundee reads and writes the following Apple HealthKit data:

  • Menstrual cycle data — used to provide cycle-phase-aware training recommendations
  • Workout data — we write completed workout sessions to Apple Health

HealthKit data is stored only on your device and in your private iCloud account. We never transmit HealthKit data to external servers, and we never use it for advertising or marketing purposes.

Workout & Training Data

Your workout logs, one-rep maxes, benchmark scores, injury records, and program enrollment are stored locally on your device using SwiftData. If you are signed in with Apple, this data syncs privately via iCloud (CloudKit Private Database) to your other devices. This data is accessible only to you.

AI-Generated Workouts

If you use the AI Workout Builder, your fitness preferences and workout parameters are sent to our cloud service to generate personalized workouts. No personally identifiable information or HealthKit data is included in these requests.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide cycle-phase-aware training recommendations
  • Track your workout history, progress, and personal records
  • Modify exercises based on injury status
  • Generate personalized AI workouts
  • Sync your data across your devices via iCloud

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect analytics or usage tracking data
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or ad tracking
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties
  • We do not transmit HealthKit data to any external server

4. Data Storage & Security

All personal data is stored either locally on your device or in your private iCloud account (CloudKit Private Database). iCloud data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple. We do not operate our own servers for storing personal user data.

Program content (workout templates and schedules) is delivered via CloudKit Public Database and bundled resources. This content does not contain personal information.

5. Subscriptions

Sundee Fundee offers optional subscriptions (Sundee Plus and Sundee Premium) through Apple's In-App Purchase system. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data remains on your device and in your iCloud account for as long as you use the app. To delete your data:

  • Delete the app from your device to remove local data
  • Use Settings > Apple ID > iCloud to manage your iCloud data
  • Contact us at support@sundeefundee.com for assistance with data deletion

7. Children's Privacy

Sundee Fundee is not directed at children under 17. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by updating the effective date below.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us at:
support@sundeefundee.com
